## Environment startup does not complete

Refresh the page and try again. If the issue persists, the required image may not be available on your institution's server. An administrator can check its status in the image marketplace.

## A published training module is not visible

Check whether the module is still a draft, its start date has passed, and your class has been added. These settings must be updated by the teacher.

## A submitted report needs correction

A report cannot be edited by students or teachers after submission. This version does not support returning a report for revision. Contact your platform administrator if it must be submitted again.

## Retaining environment data

Environment data is removed when the environment is destroyed. Copy any materials you need to retain to your own device or cloud storage beforehand.

## Password reset

Contact your institutional administrator to reset the password. Change the initial password after your first login.
