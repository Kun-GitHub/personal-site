## “Open environment” spins forever

Reload and try again. If it still fails, the image is most likely not ready on your school's server — only an administrator can fix that. Ask them to check the entry's status in the image marketplace.

## I can't see the training my teacher mentioned

Three usual causes: the module is still a draft, its start date has not arrived, or your class was not added. All three are fixed on the teacher's side.

## I submitted my report and it's wrong

Submission locks it for both students and teachers. Returning a report is not supported in this version; contact your platform administrator if it really needs to be redone.

## Can I keep the data from my environment?

No. Environments are rebuildable and cleaned up when destroyed. Copy anything you need to your own machine first.

## I forgot my password

Ask your campus administrator to reset it. Change the initial password right after your first login.
