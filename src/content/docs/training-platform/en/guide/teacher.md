title: Build a module
description: Teacher view — from creating a module to publishing, opening environments and grading.

A module holds several tasks and their resources. Once published, each student claims their own environment and works inside it. Here is the whole loop, using “Software Testing · Week 3” as the example.

## 1. Create the module

Go to **Modules → New** and fill in the name, the date range and the class it targets. A new module stays a draft, invisible to students.

## 2. Add tasks and resources

1. On the module page, click **Add task** and write the requirement and the expected deliverable;
2. Upload handouts and templates under **Resources** — students download them inside the environment;
3. Reorder with the arrows; students see that order.

> Name the deliverable: one test report, a set of defect records. Grading follows those items.

## 3. Create groups

Groups organise students and receive group-level switches; environments are per student, one independent container each, not shared by the group. Pulling a whole class in is fastest; you can also assign students one by one.

## 4. Publish and open

Publishing makes the module visible under **My training**. Students click **Open environment** and usually have a URL and account within 30 seconds.

> Open one instance yourself before the class: check the account works, the data is what you expect, and defect switches apply per group.

## 5. Grade

Reports lock on submission. Grade each one, leave a comment, and students get notified. Export the grade sheet at the end of term.
